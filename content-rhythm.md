# Content Rhythm

A weekly posting plan template for founders on LinkedIn — plus four post format templates you can reuse without sounding templated.

## Principles

- **Quality over volume.** 3–5 strong personal posts + 1–2 company page posts per week is a full rhythm for most founders.
- **Spaced, never bursty.** One post per session, hours or days apart. If two posts go out minutes apart, you're doing it wrong.
- **Personal leads, company supports.** Story and lessons from the personal profile; offers and announcements from the page.
- **Draft off-platform.** Write in a doc, review with fresh eyes, publish by hand.

Adjust the days to your market — the pattern that matters is **alternating effort days with engagement/rest days.**

## Post Format Templates

### 1. Myth vs. Fact

```
Myth: [The belief your audience holds]

Fact: [The plain correction]

[2–4 short lines explaining why the myth exists and what to do instead]

[One-line takeaway]

What are you seeing out there — [question to invite replies]?
```

*Works for:* tax myths, formation myths, banking myths, "everyone says you need X" topics.

### 2. Deadline Alert

```
[Deadline] is [date]. Here's what that actually means for [audience].

Who must file: [plain list]
What happens if you miss it: [real consequence, no exaggeration]
What to do this week: [3 concrete steps]

[Close: offer to answer questions in the comments]
```

*Rules:* only real deadlines, real consequences, dates double-checked before posting.

### 3. Lesson Learned

```
[Timeframe] ago, I [did the thing / made the mistake].

What happened: [2–3 honest lines]
What it cost or taught me: [the real lesson]
What I do differently now: [the system or rule]

[Closing line that hands the lesson to the reader]
```

*Works for:* credibility more than any other format. Specifics beat generalities — name the situation, not just the moral.

### 4. Client Story (With Permission)

```
[Client type — e.g. "A SaaS founder in Dubai"] came to us with [problem].

The situation: [what was actually going on, no confidential details]
What we did: [steps, in order]
The result: [outcome, only as specific as the client approved]

[The broader lesson for readers in the same position]
```

*Rules — non-negotiable:*
- Written permission from the client before posting. Every time.
- No names, company names, or identifying details unless the client explicitly approves them.
- No invented results. If the outcome is still in progress, say so.

## Monthly Rotation Check

At month end, tally your posts:

- [ ] At least 2 myth-vs-fact posts
- [ ] At least 1 deadline/practical-alert post (in season)
- [ ] At least 2 lesson/story posts
- [ ] Company page carried offers and proof — not copies of personal posts
- [ ] Every post was checked ~25 hours later for replies (none left unanswered)
- [ ] Best performer noted — its format gets reused next month with a new topic

## What Not to Do

- ❌ Posting the same content to personal profile and company page on the same day
- ❌ Engagement pods, mass-tagging, or "comment for the algorithm" requests
- ❌ Three posts in one hour because you "finally had time"
- ❌ Announcing a credential before it is earned and added to the profile

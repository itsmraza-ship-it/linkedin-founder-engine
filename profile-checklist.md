# Profile Checklist

A checkbox teardown of a founder's LinkedIn profile. Work top to bottom — the top of the profile earns (or loses) trust in the first five seconds.

Score yourself honestly. Anything unchecked is a task, in priority order.

## Photo & Cover

- [ ] Profile photo: current, clear head-and-shoulders, face well-lit and large in the frame
- [ ] Photo matches how you appear on video calls and at events (people should recognise you)
- [ ] No group photo, logo, sunglasses, or heavy filter as a personal photo
- [ ] Cover image uploaded at **1584 × 396 px** (personal) — company page: **1128 × 191 px**
- [ ] Cover states who you help + outcome (or a clean brand line), readable on mobile
- [ ] Cover text avoids the corners / photo-overlap area
- [ ] Cover and photo look like the same brand (colours, tone)

## Headline

- [ ] Uses the formula: **role + who you help + outcome**
- [ ] Names your audience in the words they use for themselves
- [ ] Contains searchable keywords a buyer would type (service + niche)
- [ ] No buzzword soup ("visionary | ninja | thought leader")
- [ ] Still makes sense when shown alone under a comment

## About

- [ ] First 2 lines: who you help + what changes for them (visible before "see more")
- [ ] Short story: how you got here and what you've built
- [ ] Services listed plainly, in client language
- [ ] Proof included: numbers, clients served, partnerships, credentials — all verifiable
- [ ] Closes with how to reach you / what to do next
- [ ] Written in first person, warm and direct, no corporate filler
- [ ] Read aloud once — sounds like a person, not a brochure

## Featured

- [ ] Section is enabled (not empty)
- [ ] Main offer / booking / contact asset pinned
- [ ] Best-performing post or piece of content pinned
- [ ] One proof asset pinned (guide, media, announcement, or resource)
- [ ] All Featured links tested and live
- [ ] Rotated in the last 90 days

## Experience

- [ ] Current founder role listed first, with correct dates and location settings
- [ ] Role description repeats your positioning — outcomes and who you serve, not just duties
- [ ] Media attached to the current role (links, decks, certificates, pages)
- [ ] Past roles support the story (relevant experience highlighted, clutter trimmed)
- [ ] Company page correctly linked so the logo shows

## Credentials

- [ ] All earned certifications/licenses listed under Licenses & Certifications with issuer + date
- [ ] Credential IDs / verification links added where the issuer provides them
- [ ] A queue list exists for credentials earned but not yet added
- [ ] Adds are paced — **max 2 per day**, never a bulk dump
- [ ] Education entries complete and accurate
- [ ] Volunteer / community roles added where genuine

## Contact & Settings

- [ ] Contact info current: business email and website
- [ ] Custom profile URL claimed (clean name, no random numbers)
- [ ] "Services" section filled in (for profiles that offer services)
- [ ] Open-to / providing-services settings match what you actually sell
- [ ] Profile visibility settings reviewed — public enough to be found
- [ ] Creator mode / topics set to your actual subject areas, if used

## Company Page (If You Run One)

- [ ] Logo crisp, tagline states the offer in one line
- [ ] Cover at 1128 × 191 px, consistent with personal profile branding
- [ ] About/overview complete: who, what, where, how to buy
- [ ] Website and contact buttons working
- [ ] Page posts are offers, proof, and announcements — not duplicates of personal posts

## Final Pass

- [ ] View your profile as a stranger (logged-out / "View as member") — does it earn trust in 10 seconds?
- [ ] Ask one honest peer: "From this profile alone, what do I sell and who for?" Fix whatever they get wrong.
